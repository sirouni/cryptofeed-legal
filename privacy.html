Privacy Policy

Effective 7 August 2026 · Product: Floating Crypto (iOS)

This policy describes how Floating Crypto handles information related to public market quotes, local preferences, and Picture in Picture. Using the app means you understand the practices below. If you do not, stop using the app.

What we collect

Floating Crypto does not offer accounts and does not sell personal data. Preferences stay on your device. Showing quotes makes ordinary network requests to public market-data providers.

System permissions this product does not use

This version does not use weather, GPS, Calendar, Reminders, or Apple Music / Now Playing, and it does not ask for Location, Calendar, Reminders, or media-library permission.

Network and market data

When quotes are on, the device requests public market APIs. Those providers typically see your IP address and standard HTTP metadata — the usual cost of fetching anything from the internet, not a separate sale by this app. Quotes are for display only. They are not investment advice.

What appears in Picture in Picture

The quotes you choose are rendered into a system Picture in Picture window. Anyone who can see that screen can see those quotes. The app does not upload them to a Floating Crypto server. This version has no such backend.

Silent audio and the background

While Picture in Picture is open, the app starts a silent audio session so the window can keep updating in the background. Nothing audible is played, and no audio is collected. The session uses UIBackgroundModes: audio with a zero-volume buffer. It exists only to keep Picture in Picture alive. It is not a music player and it is not used for tracking.

Analytics and tracking

This version does not include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs, and it does not use data to track you across apps.

How we use information

  • To run the app: show quotes and cast them to Picture in Picture
  • Not used for: ad targeting, data brokerage, selling personal data, or investment advice

Retention

  • Preferences stay on the device (for example in UserDefaults)
  • Uninstalling the app usually deletes those preferences with it
  • Third-party quote services keep their own request logs under their own policies

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • You can close Picture in Picture or leave the related flow at any time
  • You can stop using the app at any time

Contact

Privacy questions: GitHub Issues, sirouni@gmail.com, or About → Support in the app.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes update the effective date and, where we can, a note in the app or on the support channel. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026-08-07 · 适用产品：Floating Crypto（iOS）

本政策说明 Floating Crypto 如何处理与网络行情、本地偏好及画中画（Picture in Picture）相关的信息。使用本应用即表示你理解下文所述做法。若你不同意，请停止使用本应用。

我们收集什么

Floating Crypto 不提供账号注册，也 不出售个人数据。偏好与循环配置保存在你的设备本地；行情展示会向公开市场数据提供方发起常规网络请求。

本产品不依赖的系统权限

当前版本 不以 天气、GPS、日历、提醒事项或 Apple Music / 正在播放 等作为产品功能，因此 不会 申请定位、日历、提醒事项或媒体库等对应系统权限。

网络与行情数据

启用行情展示时，设备会向公开行情接口发起网络请求。提供方通常会收到你的 IP 地址及标准 HTTP 元数据——这是访问互联网内容的常规方式，并非本应用另行出售数据。行情仅供展示，不是投资建议。

画中画中展示的数据

你选择展示的行情会渲染到系统画中画窗口。画中画内容对同一设备上能看见屏幕的人可见；本应用不会因此将数据上传到自有服务器（当前版本无此类后端）。

静音音频与后台

为在后台维持画中画更新，开启画中画时会激活静音音频会话；不用于播放可听内容或收集音频。该会话通过 UIBackgroundModes: audio 与零音量缓冲实现，只服务于画中画连续性，不是音乐播放，也不用于跟踪。

分析与跟踪

当前版本 不集成 第三方广告或用户跟踪 SDK，也 不将数据用于跨 App 跟踪。

我们如何使用信息

  • 应用功能：驱动行情展示与画中画投送
  • 不用于：广告定向、数据经纪、出售个人数据、投资顾问服务

数据保留

  • 偏好设置保存在设备本地（如系统 UserDefaults）
  • 卸载应用后，本地偏好通常随应用数据一并删除
  • 第三方行情服务按其自身政策处理网络请求日志

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童定向设计或营销。

你的选择

  • 可随时关闭画中画或退出相关流程
  • 可随时停止使用本应用

联系我们

隐私问题请到 GitHub Issues，或发邮件至 sirouni@gmail.com，也可在应用内「关于 → 支持与反馈」。

变更

我们可能更新本政策。重大变更会更新「生效日期」，并尽量在应用或支持渠道提示。继续使用即表示接受更新后的政策。
